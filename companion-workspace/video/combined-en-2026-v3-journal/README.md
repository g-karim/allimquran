# ALLIM QURAN — Ayah of the Day Journal film

Final English master: 58.80 seconds. English with Russian voice-over: 59.80 seconds. Both are 1080 × 1920, H.264/AAC.

## Editorial structure

- The approved `shalat-211629366.webp` photograph opens the film for 4.3 seconds. It is preserved in full at the centre of the portrait frame over a softly blurred extension of the same image; the supplied watermark remains untouched.
- Real user footage from `IMG_7464.MOV`, `IMG_7465.MOV`, `IMG_7466.MOV`, and `IMG_7467.MOV` follows the opening photograph.
- Current ALLIM interface plates plus live local mobile captures of Al-Fatihah 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, and 1:5.
- Product-preview inserts for the Ayah of the Day private journal, AI-assisted takeaway, one practical action, evening review, and daily habit.
- One brief real Qur'an close-up near the end; the previous Qur'an-only emphasis was removed.
- Exact production wordmark from `homepage/allim-header-logo.png`; it was not redrawn or replaced.

## Audio

- English narrator: Microsoft `en-GB-RyanNeural`, rate `-6%`, pitch `-3Hz`.
- Qur'an reading is intentionally a different voice: complete verse-by-verse recordings of Al-Fatihah 1:2–1:5 by Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, sourced through the same EveryAyah path used by ALLIM.
- Ryan and the four complete ayat alternate throughout the film. No Qur'anic ayah is generated by the narration voice or cut in the middle.
- Original audio from the four personal MOV files is not included.
- Russian voice-over: Yandex SpeechKit v3 `anton`, neutral role, speed `1.0`. This is the third of the three voice tests approved for the final film. Speech-only stress markup uses `а+ят`, `тафс+ир`, `+Аллим`, and `Кор+ан`; the captions remain unmarked. The script avoids the rejected declined form and replaces the problematic `страны` phrase with `людей по всему миру`. Ryan's English remains audible underneath and is automatically lowered only while Anton speaks. The Al-Fatihah recordings remain the original Al-Husary audio at their original level and are not translated, regenerated, shortened, or ducked.

## Image treatment

The four iPhone HLG/Dolby Vision sources are converted to BT.709 SDR, stabilized, denoised, contrast-balanced, lightly sharpened, and exported in portrait orientation. Computer shots receive stronger stabilization and a deeper permanent upper crop: the visible picture starts inside the ALLIM content area, excluding the browser chrome, tabs, bookmarks, and unrelated VATAN/Raven workspace labels. No synthetic phone shell, floating screen replacement, or blank white phone screen is used.

## Product-status boundary

The journal, AI companion, and habit screens are clearly treated as product-preview visualizations based on the current ALLIM product direction. They are not presented as captures proving that every interaction is already live in the deployed application. The AI companion is described as a reflection aid, not as tafsir and not as a replacement for a qualified teacher.
