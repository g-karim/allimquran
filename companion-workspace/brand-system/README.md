# ALLIM QURAN — Brand System v1

> Status: visual exploration v4. The new Hadith Embrace symbol is derived from the user's hand sketch. It removes both heart shapes and replaces the earlier closed oval with two crossing gestures around an open Book.

## Brand idea

ALLIM is a Qur'an learning companion built around teaching, care and transmission of knowledge. The symbol abstracts an embrace without depicting any person: two outer forms protect and hold two inner pages. The gold central line represents knowledge being passed on.

## Logo architecture

1. **Primary logo:** symbol + `ALLIM QURAN`. Use for the website header, onboarding, documents and social profiles.
2. **Expressive display mark:** the existing `QURAN`-inside-`ALLIM` artwork. Use at large sizes, in launch films and motion; do not use it as the smallest navigation logo.
3. **App icon:** selected open-embrace symbol on deep emerald.

## Symbol concepts

- **A — Embrace Page (rejected):** the closed oval and central split created an unwanted coffee-bean association.
- **B — Knowledge Knot:** warmer and more relational, but visually more complex.
- **C — Q Embrace:** connects directly to the letter Q, but is closer to common lettermark territory.

## Color

- Deep Emerald `#0B3D33` — primary identity and digital surfaces.
- Warm Ivory `#F4F0E7` — preferred light background.
- Muted Gold `#C8A96B` — knowledge/light accent; never the main body copy color.
- Ink `#101513` and White `#FFFFFF` — monochrome production versions.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Primary lockup clear space: at least half the symbol width on all sides.
- Symbol minimum digital size: 20 px. Use the monochrome version below 28 px.
- Horizontal logo minimum width: 160 px.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, outlines or rotate the symbol in the core identity.

## Typography direction

- Latin UI: a restrained geometric sans with open counters and medium weight.
- `QURAN`: narrow uppercase display lettering derived from the internal carved form.
- Arabic: a contemporary Naskh/Kufi pairing must be drawn and reviewed as a separate native lockup; it should not be mechanically mirrored from the Latin mark.

## Motion principle

The outer forms close gently like an embrace. A central line of light appears, opens into two pages, and only then reveals the narrow `QURAN` lettering. Qur'anic text may flow across the surface as light, but should remain respectful, fully legible where shown, and never be treated as debris or destruction.
