# ALLIM — locked QURAN geometry

The embedded `Q · U · R · A · N` cutouts are immutable master geometry.

- Do not move any cutout up, down, left or right.
- Do not scale, stretch, compress, shorten, extend, rotate or redraw a cutout.
- Do not fill, cover or interrupt any part of a cutout.
- Every colour version must show the background through the cutouts.
- Future refinements may change only the outside silhouette of `ALLIM`.
- Exporters must compare the protected QURAN regions with the master mask and fail if they differ.
- Edge cleanup may remove translucent raster halos, but must preserve the locked coordinates and contours.

Master mask: `approved-logo/allim-white-transparent.png`.
