# ALLIM homepage draft

Standalone, dependency-free homepage prepared for review before copying into the website editor.

## Preview

Open through the project server:

`http://localhost:4174/homepage/?v=1`

## Product-claim boundary

The page deliberately labels the Qur’anic speech engine and AI recitation feedback as in development. Do not remove that boundary until end-to-end recitation recognition has been verified against the intended corpus, browsers/devices and real reading sessions.

The page uses a neutral text-based ALLIM identity. Existing exploratory wordmarks in `assets/branding/` are not treated as adopted brand assets.

## Access model

- Self-guided digital learning is presented as free worldwide.
- Teacher-led Academy programs may be paid because they use live teacher time, assessment and support.
- Ijazah is never described as an automated platform award; it remains subject to a qualified authorized teacher and the required evaluation.

## Share and Academy introduction

- The public share action uses the native Web Share API and falls back to copying the canonical ALLIM URL.
- Spiritual reward is expressed only as hope in Allah; the interface never promises, counts or assigns an afterlife reward.
- The Academy introduction is planned as single-level: an eligible inviter and a new paying learner may each receive 10% off one billing period after the first payment is final.
- There is no cash payout, joining fee, second level, team structure or income from later invitations.
- The general “Share ALLIM” invitation works now. The separate Academy invitation button is a disabled placeholder, and the automatic 10% discount is visibly marked as in development and is not accrued yet.
- Referral attribution, anti-abuse checks and billing credit must be implemented server-side before the discount is activated publicly.
