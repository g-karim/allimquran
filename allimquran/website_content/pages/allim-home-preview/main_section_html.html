One ayah. One complete journey.

More than detecting a mistake. More than displaying a page.

ALLIM’s goal is to unite the disciplines that are usually scattered across different apps: listening, recitation, hifz, language, tafsir, reflection and review.

01

Listen with attention

Follow a trusted recitation, repeat a selected range and absorb the rhythm before recall.

02

Recite and correct

The planned Qur’anic speech engine follows word by word, pauses at an error and resumes only after correction.

03

Understand the ayah

See vocabulary, roots, structure, translation and source-bound tafsir without losing the reading flow.

04

Carry it into life

Turn meaning into a personal reflection, a small action and a review that returns at the right time.

Qur’anic intelligence, with boundaries

An AI companion that knows when to listen — and when not to invent.

ALLIM is designed around verified Qur’anic text, clear source attribution and honest uncertainty. It should help the learner navigate trusted knowledge, never present a generated guess as tafsir or a machine score as a teacher’s ijazah.

Ijazah remains a human trust.

The platform never awards ijazah automatically. A pathway may be completed only through a qualified, authorized teacher and the required recitation, study and evaluation.

Trust before scale

Technology should serve the transmission of the Qur’an — not blur its sources.

Verified text first

Qur’anic text and learning material are connected to identifiable sources and reviewed corpora.

Visible attribution

Arabic source, translation and authorship stay visible. When a card is not verified, ALLIM should say so.

Privacy with choice

Progress should remain private by default, with clear controls for voice processing and data sync.

Teacher remains central

ALLIM supports practice between lessons. It does not replace a qualified teacher, living correction or ijazah.
